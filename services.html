Services — Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with small businesses and ministries?

Yes. LightSource Digital specializes in serving churches, ministries, and small businesses. We tailor scope and pacing to your needs and budget.

How long does a new website take?

Typical timelines:

  • Single-page site: ~1–2 weeks
  • Multi-page site: ~3–6 weeks

Timing depends on content readiness and feedback speed.

What’s included in a Website Security Audit?

A one-time review covering SSL/HTTPS, software versions, vulnerable plugins, admin access, backups, uptime, and basic performance. You receive a branded PDF report with prioritized fixes.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance or support?

Absolutely. We offer optional care plans for updates, monitoring, backups, and content tweaks. Ad-hoc support is also available.

Can you help with social media and creative media?

Yes. We design graphics, flyers, and branding assets, and we can plan content calendars or manage accounts to grow engagement.

Do you offer a free consultation?

Yes—start with a free discovery call to clarify goals and scope. Request a consultation.

Are projects remote or onsite?

Most work is remote; onsite work may be available by arrangement. Device care and troubleshooting can be done in person or via secure remote tools.

How do I get a quote?

Tell us about your project and timelines here: Contact LightSource Digital. We’ll respond within one business day.
